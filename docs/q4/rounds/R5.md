augmentation drops its mass on the padding row / column of the wall planes (not actions; the loss masks them anyway).

## 3. Loss terms

Notation: `gw` = C25 (row weight). Every term is summed over rows like the Duel terms; "Duel" means
`metrics_dict_batchwise_single_heads_output_quoridor` in `python/katago/train/metrics_pytorch.py`.

| Term (log key) | Prediction / target | Weight in `loss_sum` | Row weight | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| search policy (`p0loss`) | `policy[:,0]` / C0 normalized | 1.0 | `gw · C26` | Duel `loss_policy_player`, upstream `policy_opt_loss_scale` not used (as Duel) |
| next-seat policy (`p1loss`) | `policy_aux[:,0]` / C2 | 0.15 (inside `loss_policy_opponent_samplewise`) | `gw · C28` | Duel / upstream opponent policy |
| soft search policy (`p0softloss`) | `policy_aux[:,1]` / C0^0.25 renormalized | 0.05 × `soft_policy_weight_scale` | `gw · C26` | Duel (upstream: 1.0 × scale; Duel changed it) |
| soft next-seat policy (`p1softloss`) | `policy_aux[:,2]` / C2^0.25 | 0.02 × 0.15 × scale | `gw · C28` | Duel |
| style policy (`pstyleloss`) | `policy[:,1]` / C1 (played action) | 0.10 | `gw · C29` | **no counterpart**: it takes the channel of Duel's long-term optimistic policy (Q4IO §5.1), whose weight 0.10 it keeps |
| value (`vloss`) | 5 logits, eliminated seats masked / C0–4 | 1.50 × `value_loss_scale` | `gw · (1 − C34)` | Duel 1.50 (upstream 1.20) |
| TD value (`tdvloss1..4`, sum `tdvloss`) | `td_value[:,h]` masked / C5–9, C10–14, C15–19, C20–24 | 0.20 × 0.25 per horizon | `gw · (1 − C33)` | Duel (4 horizons including the search value; upstream trains 3) |
| plies to the end (`rtloss`) | `misc[:,0]` / C35 / 100, Huber δ 0.75 | 5/9 | `gw · C27` | Duel remaining plies: 5.0 × Huber(δ 0.25) on plies / 300 = the same loss per ply on plies / 100 |
| final distances (`fdistloss`) | `32 · misc[:,1:5]` / C36–39 (cells), Huber δ 3 | ~~0.054 per seat~~ **changed in R6 (R6.md §3.1): 0.054 / (number of seats with C40–43 > 0)** | `gw · C40–43` | Duel lead (0.054, δ 3), per Q4IO §5.2; with the division a row's total is Duel's single lead term |
| short-term value error (`evstloss`) | `0.25 · softplus(0.5 · misc[:,5])²` / (u_pred − u_real)² + 1e-8, Huber δ 0.4 | 2.0 | `gw · (1 − C33)` | Duel / upstream `loss_shortterm_value_error_samplewise`; KataGo's `win − loss` replaced by the Plan §8.3 utility of the mover `2 v[me] + (2/n) v[draw] − 1` (short-term TD horizon C15–19, prediction from the masked TD head, detached) |
| trajectory (`trajloss`) | `trajectory` / valueTargets C0, BCE mean over cells | 0.02 | `gw · C27` | Duel trajectory |
| paths (`pathsloss`) | `paths` / C0–3, BCE mean over cells, mean over seats alive at this row | 0.02 | `gw · C27` | Duel trajectory |
| future walls (`wallloss`) | `walls[:, :, :10, :10]` / C4–11, BCE mean over the anchor domain, mean over alive seats | 0.02 | `gw · C27` | Duel wall graph (anchor-domain mean) |

- **Masking.** Value and TD logits of eliminated seats (alive flags = global inputs 8–11, already relative)
  are replaced by −10000 before the softmax (`masked_fill`, so the gradient to them is exactly 0); the draw is always
  allowed. Policy logits outside the valid slots (plane 0: all 121 cells; planes 1–2: the 10 × 10 anchor grid) are
  replaced by −10000, as Duel does; legality beyond that comes from the target only, as in KataGo.
- **No Q4 counterpart (not trained):** upstream / Duel optimistic policies (the style policy took the slot), utility
  score, its stdev, lead, variance time, short-term score error, q-values, ownership / scoring / seki / score belief.
- `vsquare` = mean squared mover utility of the value head; `pacc1` = top-1 accuracy of the search policy.

## 7. Open questions

1. **One-hot search-policy targets (C++, found in R5). Fixed in R6 (R6.md §1): `getExploreSelectionValueInverse` had swapped parameters.** In every self-play row I checked (b1c32_q4 at 16 visits,
   random b2c64_q4 at 100 visits), `policyTargetsNCMove[:,0]` has exactly one nonzero slot and C32 (search entropy)
   is 0. `extractPolicyTarget` → `Search::getPlaySelectionValues` (`cpp/q4/search/q4searchresults.cpp`) keeps only one
   child; the suspect is the root play-selection reduction (`getReducedPlaySelectionWeight` /
   `getExploreSelectionValueInverse`, e.g. the utility perspective). With a random net KataGo keeps many children. Not
   fixed here (C++ search, not this round's scope); a separate task was proposed. Training on such data learns a
   one-hot search target instead of a visit distribution.
2. **TD heads: 4, not 3.** The prompt asks for KataGo's 3 TD heads; Duel trains 4 (the 4th is the search value, KataGo
   C16–19), and Plan §9 says "TD value at KataGo's four horizons". Q4 follows Duel (C20–24 as the 4th horizon).
3. **Style-policy weight 0.10** has no KataGo / Duel counterpart (§3). Round 6 uses this head; the weight may need
   revisiting then.
4. **Final-distance loss is per seat (4 × 0.054). Fixed in R6 (R6.md §3.1): divided by the number of weighted seats.** Q4IO §5.2 says "comparable to Duel's lead loss"; summed over
   seats it is up to 4 times Duel's single lead term. It dominates the loss at initialization (§4.1: 7.9 of 18.6).
5. **`test_symmetry_average_invariance_t17` guard on trained nets (§4.2):** the triviality guard should probably be
   skipped (not failed) for a checkpoint.
6. **Pre-existing, fixed in R6 (R6.md §3.3: the Q4 commands never called `Board::initHash()`):** `test_q4_parity.py` with `tf2_b4c192_q4` on `cpp/build-cuda` (RelWithDebInfo) aborts with
   `Board::init: Assertion 'IS_ZOBRIST_INITALIZED'` (4 tests); identical on `dbb573e6` (checked from a detached
   worktree with the same binary). A separate task was proposed.
