## 2. Design Decisions & Deviations

1. **Brute-Force 4-Player BFS Reachability**:
   - Rather than introducing complex biconnectivity/bridge-finding algorithms on the 11x11 grid, we implemented a 4-player BFS reachability check. Because the 11x11 board contains only 121 cells, an optimized queue BFS with bitset visited tracking runs in a few microseconds. In Release builds, move generation speed is **~18,700 generations/sec**, exceeding the round requirements and avoiding subtle corner-case bugs in cut-vertex maintenance.
2. **Snapshot-Based Undo in `Q4History`**:
   - `Q4History` stores `Q4Board` snapshots alongside move records. This guarantees $O(1)$ undo time and simplifies elimination handling (which irreversibly modifies active seat turn orders and removes pawns).
3. **Wall and Orientation Symmetry Mapping**:
   - In dihedral symmetry transformations, horizontal and vertical walls swap under 90-degree rotations and diagonal reflections. Coordinates transform with proper coordinate offsets for 10x10 wall anchors on the 11x11 board. Group axioms ($g_i \circ g_j = g_k$) and inverse identities are rigorously tested.
4. **Strict Exclusion of Ephemeral Files**:
   - `docs/q4/Plan.md` and `Q4_Round1_Prompt.md` are strictly excluded from git tracking per project guidelines.

## 6. Open Questions & Suggestions for Round 2

1. **Relative vs. Absolute Feature Planes**:
   - In 2-player Duel, current player is canonicalized to South. In 4-player Q4, canonicalizing to current player's perspective requires rotating the board by 0, 90, 180, or 270 degrees. We should standardize whether feature planes are strictly canonicalized to Seat 0 (current player) or encoded with explicit player identity planes.
2. **Spatial Action Representation**:
   - Q4 has 4 pawn moves, jump extensions, and 100 horizontal + 100 vertical wall actions (total policy size $\approx 208$ or spatial channels $11 \times 11 \times C$). We need to specify the exact policy channel mapping for symmetry rotation equivariance.
3. **Distance Transform Optimization**:
   - If feature extraction requires all 4 players' full distance maps at every MCTS expansion, vectorized bitboard operations or incremental BFS updates should be benchmarked against full BFS.
