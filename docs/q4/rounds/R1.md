## 1. File-by-File Summary of Implementation

### 1.1 Specification & Documentation
- [`docs/q4/Q4Rules.md`](file:///home/kenny/sync/HW/Quoridor/KataQuoridor/.gemini/gemini-q4-four-at-a-table/docs/q4/Q4Rules.md): Formal specification of Four-at-a-Table rules based on `GameRules.md`:
  - 11x11 board; coordinate system: columns `a`..`k` (never skipping `i`), rows `1`..`11`, center goal cell `f6` (`(5, 5)`, index 60).
  - 4 seats: Seat 0 = South (`f1`), Seat 1 = West (`a6`), Seat 2 = North (`f11`), Seat 3 = East (`k6`).
  - Turn order: clockwise South (0) → West (1) → North (2) → East (3) → South (0), skipping eliminated seats. Seat 0 moves first.
  - 7 walls per player by default (`initialWalls` range 0..10).
  - Winning condition: a pawn move ending on `f6` wins immediately (step, straight jump, diagonal jump, or two-pawn jump), or being the last alive seat.
  - Pawn jump mechanics:
    - Straight jump over an adjacent opponent pawn if unoccupied and unobstructed.
    - Diagonal jump allowed only when the straight jump is blocked by the board edge or a wall behind the jumped pawn. If the cell behind is occupied by a pawn, diagonal jumps are forbidden.
    - Two-pawn straight jump over two consecutive pawns $C_1, C_2$ into $C_3$, permitted only if every ordinary legal move strictly increases distance to the center (`dist(dest) > dist(C0)`), or there are no ordinary legal moves.
  - Elimination semantics: external event `eliminate <seat>`, removes pawn, discards walls in hand, keeps placed walls on board, passes turn to next alive seat if the eliminated seat was to move.
  - KataQuoridor extensions: `maxPlies` draw (default 400), `repetitionDrawCount` N (default 0 = off; draw on the N-th occurrence of a position since the last wall placement or elimination).

  - Bitset horizontal/vertical wall grids (`std::bitset<100>`).
  - Incremental edge-blocking table (`uint8_t blocked[121]`) for $O(1)$ adjacent edge query.
  - Complete pawn movement logic: orthogonal steps, straight single jumps, diagonal jumps (when blocked by wall or board edge behind jumped pawn), and two-pawn straight jumps under the distance constraint.

## 6. Architecture for Round 2 Part B

Per Plan §6–§7:
1. **Model Format & Header:** Option D = 101 for Q4 I/O v1.
2. **Relative Seats:** Slot 0 = me, 1 = next, 2 = across, 3 = previous (fixed slots `(toMove + k) mod 4`).
3. **Dihedral Symmetries:** All 8 board symmetries applied in tensor space; backend evaluates symmetry 0.
4. **Action Policy:** 321 actions mapped into 3 planes of 11x11 (pawn, vertical wall, horizontal wall).
5. **Heads:** Q4PolicyHead (2 channels $\times$ 3 planes: search policy and style policy) and Q4ValueHead (5 value logits, 6 misc values, 121 trajectory logits).
