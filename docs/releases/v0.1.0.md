# KataQuoridor 0.1.0

*Proposed tag: `kq-v0.1.0`.* The repository still carries upstream KataGo's tags (`v1.0` … `v1.18.x`), so
KataQuoridor tags use a `kq-` prefix to stay unambiguous.

First release of KataQuoridor, a Quoridor (Duel) engine and training pipeline based on KataGo 1.18.2. The engine
plays through QTP (GTP with Quoridor moves), trains through KataGo's self-play loop, and comes with a
match referee and Elo ladder for evaluation. This release ships a first trained net that clearly beats simple
search bots with its policy alone.

## What's in 0.1.0

This is the first release, so these notes cover everything that changed relative to upstream KataGo 1.18.2
(commit `fd0723f`), not only the last few commits.

### Game and rules

- **Quoridor Duel replaces Go.** 9 × 9 board, two players, 10 walls each. Black moves first from `e9` towards row 1;
  White starts at `e1` and heads for row 9. The rules are in [GameRules.md](../../GameRules.md).
- **Board model.** The 9 × 9 board is searched on a 17 × 17 grid:
  - pawn cells are at (even, even);
  - a horizontal wall is keyed by its centre (odd, odd);
  - a vertical wall is keyed by its upper arm (odd, even).
  That gives 81 + 64 + 64 = 209 possible moves. Placed walls are also kept as explicit 8 × 8 bitmaps
  (`Board::vWalls`/`hWalls`), which features, training targets and printing read.
- **Move rules.** Straight jumps, diagonal jumps when the straight jump is blocked by a wall or the edge, and the
  wall overlap and crossing rules.
- **No-full-block check.** Each player's cached shortest path is reused, and a full BFS runs only when a new wall
  cuts that path.
- **Game end.** `BoardHistory` ends the game the moment a pawn reaches its goal row. There is no pass, no ko and no
  engine move limit; the 300-ply cutoff lives in self-play, the gatekeeper and the arena. Go rule variations are
  gone, and the Go rule and komi config keys are optional and ignored.
- **The Quoridor margin (KataGo's "score").** When a game ends, the winner's margin is the loser's remaining
  shortest-path distance, at least 1. It is defined once (`Board::whiteMarginWhenWonBy`) and used for terminal nodes
  in search, the training target and the net's score outputs.

### QTP (GTP for Quoridor)

- **Notation.** Pawn moves are cells `a1`–`i9`; walls are `a1h`–`h8v`. `play` also accepts `move e8` / `wall e2h`.
- **New commands.** `move [color] <cell>`, `wall [color] <wall>`, `walls [color]`, `dist [color]`, `winner`,
  `legal_moves [color]`. `showboard` draws the walls, walls left, distances and the side to move.
- **Removed Go commands.** `boardsize`, `rectangular_boardsize`, `komi`, `get_komi`, `kgs-rules`, `fixed_handicap`,
  `place_free_handicap`, `set_free_handicap`, `kgs-time_settings`, `final_score`, `final_status_list`.
- **Game over is final.** Once a pawn reaches its goal, `play`, `move`, `wall`, `genmove` and all
  `genmove`/`kata-search`/analysis variants fail with `? game is over`; `legal_moves` is empty and `winner` keeps
  the result. `undo`, `clear_board`, `loadsgf` and `set_position` still work.
- **Turn order is enforced.** `play`/`move`/`wall` for the side not to move fail with
  `? illegal move: not <color>'s turn`; `genmove`, `kata-search` and `kata-analyze` for that colour fail with
  `? not <color>'s turn`. Pondering does not start on a finished game.
- **Go-only search heuristics are forced off** in every mode: anti-mirror, the MYT dagger hack, conservative pass,
  fill-dame, passing hacks, root symmetry pruning and subtree value bias.
- **Identification.** `name` returns `KataQuoridor`; `version` returns
  `KataQuoridor 0.1.0 (based on KataGo 1.18.2, git <sha>)`.

### Neural net

- **Quoridor I/O version 1** (`cpp/neuralnet/quoridornn.{h,cpp}`). All inputs are 9 × 9, in the side to move's
  perspective (the goal is always row 0).
  - 17 spatial features: on-board mask; own and opponent pawn; blocked north/south/east/west; goal row; BFS
    distances to both goal rows and from both pawns (/32); both players' shortest-path masks; placed vertical and
    horizontal wall anchors; the wall-anchor domain mask.
  - 15 global features: side to move, the two wall counts and their exponential encodings, jump-tempo parity,
    and both shortest distances.
- **Policy.** 3 planes (pawn, vertical wall, horizontal wall) of 9 × 9, mapped back to the 17 × 17 search grid.
- **Symmetries.** Only identity and left-right mirror; the input row is mirrored before it reaches the backend.
- **Model files.** KataGo architecture version 17. Header model option D is the Quoridor I/O version, and policy
  option A is the number of policy planes (3). Go nets are rejected with a clear error.
- **Outputs.**
  - Value: win/loss.
  - Game margin (as score mean and lead), margin stdev, short-term win/loss and margin errors, variance time.
  - There is no ownership output; the ownership map is zeros.
- **Backends.** CUDA and Eigen support N policy planes. OpenCL, TensorRT and Metal refuse to load a net.
- **Board-size scales.** Score utility, TD horizons, temperature half-lives, resignation, time control and fork
  timing use the real 9 × 9 board, not the 17 × 17 grid. The score-utility scale base is 9, as for 9 × 9 Go.
- **Graph search.** Transpositions are merged after wall moves (a position cannot repeat after a wall). After
  pawn moves, nodes stay path-keyed.

### Self-play, training data and training

- **Training data (npz).**
  - 9 × 9 inputs; distance channels 8–11 are stored raw in the new `spatialDistNCHW` array.
  - 3 × 81 policy targets.
  - The game-margin target and weight are in `globalTargetsNC` 21/29.
  - Four spatial auxiliary targets replace ownership: the future pawn trajectory of each side, and the final
    vertical and horizontal walls.
- **Self-play.**
  - Games that hit the 300-ply cutoff (`maxMovesPerGame`, alias `cutoffMoves`, default 300) are not written.
  - Fork candidates mix uniform, pawn-heavy and shortest-path moves.
  - New limits: `-max-valid-games-total`, `-max-rows-total` (and config `maxValidGamesTotal`, `maxRowsTotal`,
    `countOnlyValidGamesForMaxGamesTotal`).
  - Per-model logs of games started, finished and cut off, with the cutoff rate and average game length.
- **Gatekeeper.** Logs decisive games, draws by cutoff and the average game length.
- **Configs.** New `selfplay_quoridor.cfg`, `gatekeeper_quoridor.cfg` and `gtp_quoridor.cfg`.
  `synchronous_loop.sh` uses them with larger cycles (10,000 games per cycle) and `train.py -export-only-at-end`.
- **PyTorch model.**
  - `QuoridorValueHead` and an 18-output policy head (6 targets × 3 planes) with Quoridor losses.
  - Presets `tf2_b4c192_quoridor` (alias `tf3_b4c192_quoridor`) and `b2c64_quoridor`.
  - The training loader has Quoridor symmetries and rebuilds distance channels 8–11 from `spatialDistNCHW`.
- **Exporter.** Writes Quoridor nets as v17 `.bin` and `.bin.gz`, plus an optional ONNX file (policy and value only).
- **S8–S11 fix.** Distance channels 8–11 used to reach training truncated to 0/1. They are now exact, and two
  scripts upgrade older data and checkpoints (see [DistPlanesUpgrade.md](../DistPlanesUpgrade.md)):
  - `python/quoridor_add_dist_planes.py` upgrades old training data;
  - `python/quoridor_migrate_dist_planes.py` migrates old checkpoints.

### Tools

- **Arena** (`python/quoridor_arena`). Plays any QTP engines against each other, with a net-less KataQuoridor process
  as the arbiter. It uses paired random openings, is resumable, writes SGFs, and fits a Bradley-Terry Elo ladder with
  bootstrap CIs. See [Evaluation.md](../Evaluation.md).
- **Viewers.**
  - `python/sgfs_viewer`: browse self-play and arena `.sgfs` files, with per-game statistics.
  - `python/model_viewer`: per-net analysis (architecture, heads, feature importance, calibration, raw net vs
    search).
- **New `katago` subcommands.**
  - `dumpnninputs` and `evalnnparity`: the C++/PyTorch NN parity harness.
  - `writesampletrainquoridor`: synthetic training files for tests.
- **CUDA check.** `scripts/cuda_parity.sh` builds CUDA, runs FP32/FP16 NN parity and 60 plies of `genmove`.

### Tests

- **`katago runtests`** now runs the Quoridor groups (`core`, `rules`, `quoridor`, `quoridorscore`, `nninputs`,
  `trainingwrite`). Upstream Go tests are only in the opt-in `go` group. The Quoridor tests cover:
  - perft from fixed positions;
  - a lazy-BFS vs full-BFS fuzz, hashing and undo;
  - terminal margins and margin targets;
  - anti-dithering search;
  - features, symmetries and training-data encoding.
- **Python tests** (`python/tests`):
  - a reference perft;
  - NN parity between C++ and PyTorch;
  - model shapes, gradients, symmetries and ONNX;
  - distance-plane parity and the upgrade tools;
  - an end-to-end self-play → shuffle → train → export cycle;
  - QTP rule enforcement.
  The arena has its own tests with a mock engine.

## Known limitations

- **First-player advantage, no komi.** Between strong players Black (the first player) wins about 59–65% of
  games, and there is no komi or other compensation yet (planned as "Quoridor I/O v2", Phase 6 of the
  [roadmap](../KataQuoridor_Review_and_Roadmap.md)). Evaluate with colour-swapped pairs.
- **Rare 300-ply draws: the winner sometimes doesn't finish.** 38 of 5324 ladder games (0.7%) hit the 300-ply
  cutoff, mostly against early snapshots or the SimpleQuoridor search bots. In the cases examined, the
  winning side had an immediate winning move (e.g. a diagonal jump onto its goal row past a pawn blocking
  it) but kept postponing it. Once its win rate saturates at 100%, only the score (margin) utility ranks
  moves. The margin is the loser's remaining distance, so "win later after placing more walls" looks
  better than winning now. Against an opponent that just stands in the way, low-visit searches then loop.
  A fix (play an immediate win when one exists) is planned.
- **No ownership output.** The net has no ownership head, so KataGo's ownership features (e.g. `ownership` in
  analysis) are not available.
- **The gatekeeper overstates per-generation gains.** Its match results make each new net look like a bigger
  step than the arena ladder shows; use the ladder, not gatekeeper win rates, to measure progress.
- Supported backends are CUDA and Eigen only; OpenCL, TensorRT and Metal refuse to load a net, and ROCm and the
  C++ ONNX backend are untested.
- Only the Duel mode (9 × 9, two players) is implemented.
- `set_position` and SGF setup properties (`AB`/`AW`) are Go leftovers. A pawn or wall placed that way corrupts the
  board: it adds a second pawn marker or a wall that blocks nothing. Only use them with no arguments or with the
  start squares; SGFs written by KataQuoridor are fine.
- The recommended net's short-term optimistic policy (the channel search uses at `policyOptimism = 1`) was trained
  with weights taken from the longest TD horizon instead of the short-term one. This is fixed for future training.
- Some upstream subcommands are still Go-specific. `katago benchmark` aborts ("Illegal move in SGF") because it
  replays a built-in Go game, and `genconfig` is untested. The entry points used and tested so far are `gtp`,
  `selfplay`, `gatekeeper`, `runtests` and `version`.
